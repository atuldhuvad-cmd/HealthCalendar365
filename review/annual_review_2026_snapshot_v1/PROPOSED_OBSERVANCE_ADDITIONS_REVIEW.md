- **Authority / Designation:** Global Snakebite Initiative / WHO (Snakebite categorized as priority Neglected Tropical Disease; observed Sep 19 annually since 2018) & MoHFW National Action Plan for Prevention and Control of Snakebite Envenoming (NAPSE).

- **Core Clinical Focus:** Bite prevention in agricultural/rural settings, immediate immobilization and strict avoidance of harmful native practices (no cuts, no suction, no tourniquets), rapid 108 transport, in-hospital ASV administration and 20WBCT monitoring.

#### Clinical & Technical Reviewer Notes
1. **Public Health Rationale:** Adulteration of food staples directly drives both acute toxicities (e.g. epidemic dropsy from argemone oil in mustard oil, organophosphate contamination) and insidious chronic non-communicable pathology (hepatic cirrhosis from aflatoxins, renal tubular damage from heavy metal pigments like lead chromate in turmeric, and gastrointestinal inflammation).
2. **Citizen Science via DART Protocol:** The FSSAI *Detect Adulteration with Rapid Test* (DART) compendium provides zero-chemical or low-chemical kitchen tests that empower households to detect physical contaminants (chalk, starch, detergent) without requiring laboratory infrastructure.
3. **Regulatory Grievance Redressal:** Under the Food Safety and Standards Act (2006), consumers possess the statutory right to have samples tested by accredited food testing laboratories and to register complaints directly with the Food Safety Officer (FSO) or through the national toll-free portal (1800-112-100).
4. **Placement Decision (`DEC-12`):** This 4-card set is ready for deployment either as a coexisting track with World Food Safety Day (07 June) / World Food Day (16 October), or as a routine replacement replacing one of the duplicated generic Eat Right India days in the baseline calendar.
