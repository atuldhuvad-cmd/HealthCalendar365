## 1. Gujarat Healthcare Architecture & Scheduling Framework

In Gujarat, reproductive, maternal, newborn, child, and adolescent health services operate through a structured dual-delivery framework:

### 1.1 Fixed Facility Monday Services (User-Confirmed Gujarat Local Practice)
- **Facility-Based Mamta Divas (Mondays):** Operates across five verified institutional levels:
  1. Primary Health Centres (PHCs)
  2. Community Health Centres (CHCs)
  3. Urban Primary Health Centres (UPHCs)
  4. Sub-district Hospitals (SDHs)
  5. District Hospitals
- **Service Scope:** Medical Officer consultations, obstetric ultrasound linkages, routine laboratory investigations, high-risk pregnancy (HRP) management, specialist referrals, and facility-based immunization.

### 1.2 Rural Outreach Wednesday Sessions (Mamta Divas / VHSND)
- **Community Mamta Sessions (Wednesdays):** Organized weekly across village Anganwadi Centres and Sub-centres by Auxiliary Nurse Midwives (ANMs), Accredited Social Health Activists (ASHAs), and Anganwadi Workers (AWWs) in accordance with village population thresholds and block microplans (typically 1st, 2nd, 3rd, or 4th Wednesday of each month).
- **Service Continuum & Operational Distinctions:**
  1. **Session Services (On-Site Point-of-Care):** Rapid clinical assessment, antenatal registration and MCP card issuance, blood pressure measurement, abdominal palpation, digital point-of-care hemoglobin testing, urine albumin/sugar screening, Td/TT immunization, child immunization as per UIP, Vitamin A administration, and contraceptive distribution according to local microplans and available trained staff/supplies.
  2. **Counselling:** One-on-one and group communication regarding birth preparedness, exclusive breastfeeding, complementary feeding demonstrations (Annaprashan), dietary iron synergy, danger sign recognition, personal hygiene, and modern spacing methods.
  3. **Community Home Visits:** Outreach conducted by ASHAs at the doorstep:
     - **HBNC:** First 42 days (6 visits for institutional births; 7 visits for home births, adding Day 1).
     - **HBYC:** 3 to 15 months (5 quarterly visits at 3, 6, 9, 12, 15 months).
     - Tracking missed vaccination doses, dropout follow-up, and low-birth-weight monitoring.
  4. **Referral Pathways:** Immediate identification of danger signs or clinical complications (severe pallor, high blood pressure, obstructed labour, neonatal distress, severe acute malnutrition with medical complications) and coordination of rapid transfer via `108` emergency ambulance or structured referral to Ayushman Arogya Mandirs, CHCs, First Referral Units (FRUs), or District Early Intervention Centres (DEIC).
