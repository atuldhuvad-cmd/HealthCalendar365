ℹ️ Review Console Legend & Governance Guidelines

Governance Boundaries

  • Active Calendar Immutability: The active poster studio files (health_365.json, approved observances, and overrides) remain 100% frozen. The review view consumes a separate review payload.
  • Visual Status Separation: Existing photos are labeled CURRENT_ACTIVE_ASSET. Proposed visual briefs are labeled PROPOSED_BRIEF_AWAITING_GENERATION and must never be considered approved matches for revised copy.
  • Offline Access: All 365 dates and review dossiers are bundled locally into the PWA service worker precache. External guideline links require internet.

Clinical Status Badges

  • CLINICAL COPY APPROVED: Previously signed-off campaigns (Items 1–24 and Item 38).
  • PENDING CLINICAL SIGN-OFF: Draft observance campaigns (Batches 3, 4, 5), routine replacement sets (Sets 1–16), and climate-health additions.
  • PROPOSED WORDING CORRECTION: Retained baseline topics with identified clinical, numerical, device-specificity, or absolute-claim errors (DEC-14 to DEC-19).
  • RETAINED SOUND BASELINE: Baseline topics audited against NHM/CPHC standards and confirmed clinically sound.
