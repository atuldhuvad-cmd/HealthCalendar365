## Executive Summary of Identified Discrepancies

Across the 368 days of the Health Calendar Studio, image discrepancies fall into 5 distinct architectural tiers:
1. **Gender / Subject Misalignment:** (e.g. Male on World Menopause Day, Exercise photo on Gynecologist Advice card).
2. **Cross-Topic Contamination / Direct Swaps:** (e.g. Toothbrush/teeth in Ear Care, Ear Anatomy in Toothbrushing, Lungs in Earbuds card, Cotton bud in Anti-Tobacco card).
3. **Missing Clinical Topic Artifacts:** (e.g. No Iodized Salt packaging/jars on World Iodine Day; generic fridge/salad instead. No Indian gutkha/bidi/tobacco pouches on Tobacco card).
4. **Demographic / Cultural Inauthenticity:** (e.g. Western/Caucasian stock models, lack of authentic Indian couples, missing certified ISI full-face motorcycle helmets and prominent seatbelts).
5. **Universal Placeholder Defaulting (358 Days / 1,432 Cards):** (97.3% of the calendar relies on 5 generic repeating category placeholders: `cat_cardio.png`, `cat_infection.png`, `cat_mental.png`, `cat_nutrition.png`, `cat_yoga.png`).

## Root Cause Analysis & Preventive Protocol

### 1. Root Cause Analysis

- **Sprite Sheet Inversion:** Monolithic 51 artwork sprite sheets were initially sliced using blind numerical indices, causing cross-contamination (e.g. `art-hearing` and `art-oral-health` quadrants inverted).
- **Subject Neutrality Blindspots:** Elderly male assets were cropped for bone health without accounting for gender-specific observances (Menopause Day).
- **Topic Blind Fallbacks:** When dedicated assets were missing for World Iodine Day, assets from Food Safety (refrigeration) were used as fallbacks, causing complete topical mismatch.
- **Cultural Representation Gap:** Early artwork sheets generated via non-regional prompts produced Caucasian and East Asian subjects rather than authentic Indian clinical and domestic settings.

### 2. Mandatory Quality & Asset Verification Protocol

Every poster and card must satisfy these four release gates before being marked production-ready:
1. **Gender / Physiological Match Gate:** Cards for female/male-specific topics (Menopause, Cervical Cancer, Prostate Health, Maternal Health) must strictly depict matching subjects.
2. **Direct Subject Correlation Gate:** The visual subject MUST directly depict the primary noun/verb of the card (e.g., Iodine Day MUST show iodized salt; Road Safety MUST show ISI helmets/seatbelts; Ear Care MUST NOT show teeth).
3. **Authentic Indian Context Gate:** Visuals must feature Indian families, Indian domestic kitchens, Indian road vehicles (ISI certified helmets, seatbelts), and Indian clinical environments.
4. **Zero Cross-Day Duplication Gate:** No poster may share identical images across its 4 cards, and no cross-topic image swaps are permitted.
